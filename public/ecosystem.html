Join Sparrow Ecosystem

Verify your email and choose a country to publish your profile. Device location verification is optional. Existing Sparrow accounts can sign in here.

1. Verify your email

Codes expire after two minutes. Use Resend OTP when needed; requests have a 60-second cooldown. Sparrow never asks you to share your code with another member.

Choose your country

Choose your country to continue. You can optionally try a device location check for up to three seconds. Sparrow checks the selected country against an offline country map. Laptop estimates are accepted up to 10 km uncertainty when the mapped country can be checked confidently. Exact coordinates stay private; no geocoding provider receives them.

A successful check means device GPS matched the mapped country. It does not prove nationality, residence or identity and cannot prevent GPS spoofing. Uncertain borders or unmapped areas need another capture or support. Use HTTPS or localhost.

Read Sparrow’s community terms

Version: 3 October 2026

  • This Ecosystem connects entertainment professionals, employers and creative businesses. Represent your work, experience and opportunities honestly.
  • No harassment, abusive language, body shaming, threats, fraud, impersonation or requests for another person’s password or OTP.
  • Share photos, portfolio work and content you own or have permission to use. Respect other members’ privacy.
  • Use Follow, connections and messages respectfully. Do not spam or manipulate engagement.
  • Your published profile and photos are visible to signed-in Ecosystem members. Your email, private phone and exact device coordinates are not part of your public profile.
  • Sparrow screens known text patterns and accepts reports for moderator review. Content or accounts may be restricted; members can request review through Account support.
  • Employers and professionals must agree their own work and payment terms. Sparrow does not guarantee employment, follower growth, payment or contract performance.
